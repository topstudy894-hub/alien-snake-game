## Full description - English
Eat, grow and survive in a wild alien arena!

Steer your alien snake, gobble fruit to grow longer, and trap other players with your body. Bump into another snake's body or the arena wall and it's game over - so stay sharp!

GAME FEATURES
• Online multiplayer - jump into Quick Play or make a private room and share the 5-letter code with friends
• Practice mode - play offline against bots, no internet needed
• 16 unique alien snakes - from the starter Alien Basic to the legendary Gangrage Snake with a dragon head, spikes and a glowing energy trail
• Earn coins every match and unlock new skins in the shop
• Power-ups: Magnet pulls food to you, Shield protects you, x2 Score doubles your points
• Boost to sprint past rivals - but it costs you length
• Daily rewards, levels, kill streaks and a live top-10 leaderboard
• Smooth touch joystick controls made for phones
• Small download and fast to start

HOW TO PLAY
1. Drag anywhere on the screen to steer.
2. Hold the BOOST button to speed up.
3. Eat fruit and food to grow longer.
4. Never touch another snake's body - make them crash into yours!

No account needed. No chat. Your coins and skins are saved on your device.

Tap Play and become the biggest alien in the arena!
