This policy explains what the game "Alien Snake" (the "game") does with information. We keep it as small as possible.

1. Information you give the game

  • Display name. When you play online, the name you type (up to 10 characters) and the skin you picked are sent to our game server and shown to the other players in your room. Do not type your real name or personal details.
  • Room code. If you create or join a private room, the 5-letter room code is processed by the server so friends can join.

2. Information stored on your device

Your coins, unlocked skins, selected skin, name, level, daily-reward streak, settings and best score are stored on your own device (browser/app storage). We do not receive or store them on a server. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game deletes them.

3. Information processed by the game server

  • While you are connected, the server receives your steering direction and boost button presses to run the match, and temporarily processes your connection (including your IP address) to deliver the game and prevent abuse, for example by limiting the number of connections per address.
  • Match state is kept only in the server's memory. We do not keep accounts, chat logs, or a database of players, and we do not write your IP address or name to permanent storage.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time) for security and operation, under its own policy.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks at this time.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling SDKs, and no sale of data.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  • There is no text chat between players.

If we add ads or analytics in a future version, this policy and the store's data-safety information will be updated first.

5. Security

Online play uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS/WSS) when the game is hosted with HTTPS. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because players can see each other's display names, parents should supervise younger players.

7. Your choices

You can play the offline Practice mode without sending anything to a server. You can delete all locally stored data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. Because we do not keep player accounts, there is no server-side profile to delete; for any question or request, contact us below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
